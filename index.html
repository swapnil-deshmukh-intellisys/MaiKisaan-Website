Maikisaan is an innovative e-commerce platform transforming agriculture by connecting farmers, suppliers, and consumers.
Our mission is to deliver timely, affordable services across the country, with a vision to improve farmer livelihoods, enhance market access, and create a transparent, efficient system for all.
With features like direct farmer connections, a trading platform for agricultural products, automated price updates, and end-to-end logistics, Maikisaan simplifies farming and business operations.
As a friend of farmers, we ensure quality assurance by promoting traceability and trust.
Maikisaan is a catalyst for innovation, driving growth and sustainability in the agricultural industry
